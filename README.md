# Inside Out

## About
*Inside Out* is a **Pixar animated movie** about a girl named Riley and the emotions that live inside her mind.

## Why It's My Favorite
1. It explains feelings in a simple, creative way.
2. It is funny and emotional at the same time.
3. It shows that **every emotion matters**, even sadness.

## The Emotions
| Emotion | Color | Personality |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Joy | Yellow | Positive and energetic |
| Sadness | Blue | Gentle and thoughtful |
| Anger | Red | Hot-headed |
| Fear | Purple | Always cautious |
| Disgust | Green | Picky and sassy |

## Movie Details
- **Year:** 2015
- **Genre:**
  - Animation
  - Comedy
  - Family

## Best Lesson
> It's okay to feel sad. Every feeling has a purpose.
